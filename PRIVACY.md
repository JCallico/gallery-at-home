# Gallery at Home: privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Gallery at Home is a photo gallery and picture-frame app for Samsung TVs. This
policy covers the version published in the Samsung TV app store.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting, and it never sends anything to the developer or to any server the
developer runs.

## What stays on the TV

To work, the app saves the following in the TV's own storage for the app:

- your collections, and which photos and folders are in them;
- the sources you add: the addresses of network shares and web links, and
  for network shares, the user name and password you type in;
- your settings and play styles, what is "now showing", and the page you
  were on (so the app can reopen there).

This information never leaves the TV. Deleting the app from the TV deletes
it.

## Who the app talks to

Only the places you choose:

- **USB drives** plugged into the TV are read directly.
- **Network shares** (a NAS or PC): the app connects to the address you enter,
  sending the user name and password you gave for that share, to list and
  show its photos.
- **Web links**: the app downloads the pages and photos at the links you
  enter.

Those services see the requests the TV makes, as they would from a web
browser (including the TV's IP address), and their own privacy policies
apply. The included photo collections are part of the app and need no
network.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
