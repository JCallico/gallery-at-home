## Short description

Turn your TV into a picture frame: your photos and classic art, framed and

## Description

Gallery at Home turns your Samsung TV into a picture frame and slideshow for
your own photos and for the collections that come with it.

YOUR PHOTOS, FROM WHERE THEY ARE
• USB drives plugged into the TV
• Network shares on your NAS or PC (WebDAV or web folders)
• Web links: photos, pages or lists of photos online
Build collections from any mix of them, one photo at a time or whole folders.

READY TO ENJOY
Seven included collections, about 56 photos in all: Masterpieces, Mountains,
Still Waters, Coastlines, Forests & Waterfalls, After Dark and Space. Every
photo is credited in the app.

A FRAME FOR EVERY ROOM
Gallery white, Modern black, Natural oak, Dark walnut, Classic gold or
Floating. The whole screen becomes the frame, with a mat cut to the photo and
a gallery-style caption and clock.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Slideshow, with crossfade, slide or zoom
• Ken Burns: slow pan and zoom
• Mosaic: a wall of seven photos, changing one at a time
• Duo: portrait photos side by side
• Memories: photos taken on this day in past years

ALWAYS SHOWING
Whatever you play last stays "now showing". Open the app and it's there; leave
the menus and it comes back after a few minutes.

EASY WITH THE REMOTE
Four Home layouts to find collections fast, including an A–Z jump bar.
Everything works with the TV remote; no phone app or account needed.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your collections and settings stay on your
TV.

## Seller information

To fill in from your Seller Office profile: seller name, support email, home
page (the GitHub repository works), and the phone number and mailing address
Samsung asks for. Use business or support contact details rather than a home
address or personal phone if you can, since the store may show them.
